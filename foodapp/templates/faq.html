Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ordering homemade meals and using KitchenStation.

Orders and Delivery

Browse the available homemade meals, select a meal you like, add it to your cart, and follow the checkout process to place your order.

Visit your Orders page to review your order history and check the status information available for your orders.

If you need to cancel an order or have a question about an existing order, please contact KitchenStation support for assistance.

Meals and Home Cooks

KitchenStation connects customers with local home cooks offering homemade meals. Customers can explore available meals and place orders through the platform.

Meal availability can vary by home cook. Check the availability information displayed for a meal before placing your order.

Open the Sign Up page and register using the Home Cook role. If you're currently logged in with a customer account, log out first so you can create a separate home-cook account.

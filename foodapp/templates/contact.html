Common Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse homemade meals on the Homemade Meals page, select a meal, choose your quantity, and proceed to checkout.

How can I find a home cook?

Visit the Home Cooks page to browse local home cooks by location and cuisine.

Can I cancel my order?

Order cancellations depend on the preparation status. Contact support as soon as possible if you need to cancel.

How do I check my order status?

You can track your order through the Order Tracking page after placing your order.

How can I contact a home cook?

At this stage, all communication with home cooks is managed through KitchenStation support. Contact us with your order details.

How do I become a home cook?

If you'd like to offer homemade food through KitchenStation, use the contact form to express your interest and we'll get back to you.
